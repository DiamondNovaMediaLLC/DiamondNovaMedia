Diamond Nova Media LLC uses this locally operated application to plan, produce and manage videos for its own authorized YouTube channels.

What the application does

  • Plans topics and creates German-language scripts, synthetic narration, animated scenes, captions and video drafts.
  • Lets an authorized operator review drafts and approve uploads or scheduled publication.
  • Reads channel and video information, audience comments and YouTube Analytics to support channel management and editorial decisions.

Google authorization

The application connects through Google's official authorization screen. It does not ask for your Google password. Access is limited to the YouTube permissions you approve. This is an internal tool for authorized channel operators; it is not a public account-registration service.

Read the application privacy notice for details about Google data, local storage, processing, access revocation and deletion.
